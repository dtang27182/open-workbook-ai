Connect OpenRouter

OpenRouter is the service that powers the AI answers and spreadsheet edits in Open Workbook AI.

  • Why you need an accountThe add-in is free, but you’ll need an OpenRouter account to pay for the AI you use.
  • New to OpenRouter?Choose “Sign in or create account” and create your account in the OpenRouter window. It connects to the add-in when you’re done.
  • Your key stays on this deviceThe OpenRouter window creates a key for this add-in. The key is stored on this device only.
  • What gets sentRequests send the active worksheet’s used range and your message to OpenRouter. Don’t use workbooks you aren’t permitted to share.
